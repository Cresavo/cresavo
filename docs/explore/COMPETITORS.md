# Booking platforms for Swedish salons and clinics

18 November 2026 · Internal research for [goal 23](../goals/QUEUE.md#23--pricing-modules-and-billing-open-core) and the [website service proposal](WEBSITE_SERVICE.md).

Listed prices are monthly unless indicated otherwise. SEK is the default currency. Foreign-market examples retain their listed currencies. Compare business prices before VAT and confirm VAT treatment where the source leaves it unclear. **(verify)** marks a historical figure, regional mismatch, ambiguous allowance, or detail that needs confirmation in the offer. Review themes come from public posts by businesses and consumers. The posts describe individual experiences, and the samples vary widely by platform and country.
