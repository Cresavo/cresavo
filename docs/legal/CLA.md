# Cresavo Contributor License Agreement

This agreement applies between the contributor identified below and the legal
person or entity identified as the Cresavo project maintainer (the
**Maintainer**). It applies to Contributions you intentionally submit after
you accept it, and also to the Contributions in the pull request or patch
for which you accept it (including commits you added to it before
accepting). Earlier work outside that pull request needs a separate
agreement. Before using this draft, identify the Maintainer, give the
agreement a version identifier, and confirm the applicable law, venue and
handling of contributor signature records.

**Status:** this is a draft for comment. The project is not collecting
signatures on it yet.

In plain words: you keep ownership of your work. The grant below lets the
Maintainer also offer your Contribution under commercial or proprietary
terms. Everyone who already received it under an open-source licence keeps
that licence.

## 1. Contributions

A **Contribution** is code, documentation or other original material,
including modifications or additions, that you intentionally submit to the
Maintainer for inclusion in Cresavo through a project contribution channel.
Material you clearly identify in writing as excluded when you submit it
falls outside this agreement. An entity may submit Contributions through
individuals it authorizes to act on its behalf.

elsewhere. You grant the Maintainer and its successors a worldwide,
perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license under your
copyright and similar rights to use, reproduce, modify, create derivative
works from, publicly display and perform, make available, distribute,
sublicense and relicense your Contributions, in source or binary form,
alone or with other work. The Maintainer may exercise those rights under
open-source, proprietary or commercial terms, including dual licensing
and open-core distribution. Existing recipients keep the rights they
received under their applicable licenses.

You confirm that you have authority to grant these rights and that, to your
knowledge, the original parts of your Contribution do not infringe third-party
rights. If an employer or other party holds rights in your Contribution,

material and its license or restrictions when you submit it. This agreement
grants only the rights you have authority to license. Tell the Maintainer
promptly if you learn that a statement you made under this agreement was
inaccurate.

## 5. Support and acceptance

You supply each Contribution as is and disclaim warranties to the fullest
extent permitted by law, except for the commitments in this agreement. You
may offer support separately. The Maintainer decides whether to accept or
