# Flerdo Contributor License Agreements

> **DRAFT – must be reviewed by a lawyer before use.**
> This text is a working draft modelled on the structure of the Apache
> Individual and Corporate Contributor License Agreements (ICLA, CCLA). It is
> not legal advice and has not been reviewed. Bracketed items need decisions.
> Do not enable the CLA Assistant with this text until a lawyer has approved
> it. Note: the project is licensed AGPL-3.0-only; if proprietary add-ons are
> ever offered, the license grant in section 2 must be reviewed for that.

The "Project" is Flerdo and the software in the repositories under the GitHub
organization `flerdo`. The "Steward" is [legal entity name and registration
number, to be decided], which receives contributions on behalf of the Project.

## Part A – Individual Contributor License Agreement

By signing (or agreeing through the CLA Assistant on a pull request), You
accept and agree to the following terms for Your present and future
Contributions submitted to the Steward.

1. **Definitions.** "You" means the individual who submits a Contribution.
   "Contribution" means any original work of authorship, including any
   modification or addition to existing work, that You intentionally submit to
   the Steward for inclusion in the Project. "Submit" means any form of
   electronic, written or verbal communication sent to the Steward or its
   representatives, including pull requests, issues and mailing lists, but
   excluding communication conspicuously marked "Not a Contribution".

2. **Copyright license.** You grant the Steward and recipients of software
   distributed by the Steward a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
   no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce,
   prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
   sublicense and distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works.
   [Decision for lawyer: whether the Steward may relicense Contributions under
   terms other than AGPL-3.0, for example for dual licensing.]

3. **Patent license.** You grant the Steward and recipients of software
   distributed by the Steward a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
   no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated here) patent license
   to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise
   transfer the Contribution, where such license applies only to those patent
   claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your
   Contribution alone or by combination with the Project. If any entity
   institutes patent litigation alleging that Your Contribution or the Project
   constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, the patent licenses
   granted to that entity under this Agreement for that Contribution
   terminate.

4. **Your rights.** You represent that You are legally entitled to grant the
   above licenses. If Your employer has rights to intellectual property that
   You create, You represent that You have received permission to make the
   Contribution on behalf of that employer, or that Your employer has waived
   such rights, or that Your employer has signed the Entity CLA (Part B).

5. **Originality.** You represent that each Contribution is Your original
   creation and, to Your knowledge, does not violate any third party's
   intellectual property rights. You will identify any third-party material
   and its license in the Contribution. You are not expected to provide
   support for Your Contributions, except to the extent You choose to. You may
   provide Contributions on an "AS IS" basis, without warranties of any kind.

6. **Personal data.** You consent to the Steward storing Your name, GitHub
   username and e-mail address, and the record of Your agreement, for as long
   as the Project exists, to prove this Agreement. [Lawyer: GDPR wording,
   legal basis, retention.]

7. **Notice.** You will notify the Steward if You become aware of any
   circumstance that makes a representation in this Agreement inaccurate.

8. **Governing law.** [Governing law and venue, to be decided with the
   lawyer.]

**Signature:** the agreement is accepted electronically through the CLA
Assistant (GitHub account, date, repository, pull request).

## Part B – Entity (Corporate) Contributor License Agreement

This Agreement is for a company, organization or other legal entity ("the
Entity") that wants its employees or contractors ("Designated Employees") to
submit Contributions to the Steward.

1. **Definitions.** As in Part A, except "You" means the Entity and all
   entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with
   it ("control" means ownership of at least 50 percent of voting interests or
   the power to direct management). "Contribution" includes any work
   submitted by a Designated Employee on behalf of the Entity.

2. **Licenses.** The Entity grants the Steward and recipients of software
   distributed by the Steward the copyright license (Part A section 2) and the
   patent license (Part A section 3) for all Contributions submitted by its
   Designated Employees, with the same terms, including patent termination.

3. **Authority.** The signatory represents that they are authorized to sign
   for the Entity, and that the Entity is legally entitled to grant these
   licenses for Contributions of its Designated Employees.

4. **Designated Employees.** The Entity maintains a list of Designated
   Employees (name, GitHub username) and will update it by notifying the
   Steward when people are added or removed. Contributions by listed persons
   are covered by this Agreement. A Designated Employee does not need to sign
   Part A separately for work done for the Entity.

5. **Originality and third-party material.** As Part A section 5.

6. **Notice.** The Entity will notify the Steward if any representation here
   becomes inaccurate.

7. **Personal data and governing law.** As Part A sections 6 and 8.

**Entity details to record:** legal name, registration number, address,
signatory name and title, contact e-mail, date, and the list of Designated
Employees.
