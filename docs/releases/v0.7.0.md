# Cresavo 0.7.0 – online booking for real salons, ready behind the scenes

> Pre-release. Concept demo with fictional data; not ready for real customers yet.

0.6.0 was about marketing. 0.7.0 is about the core of a booking system: letting a salon's own customers book, get a confirmation and change their booking themselves. All of it now exists and is tested, but it stays switched off. Real salons get it once the remaining safety and privacy checks are done, so the public demo works exactly as before.

## New since 0.6.0

- **Booking on a salon's own page.** A customer picks a treatment and a free time on the salon's page and books with name, mobile number and e-mail. Double bookings are impossible, even when many people press the button at the same moment. A customer who presses twice gets one booking. Price and cancellation terms are checked at the moment of booking, so nobody is booked on terms they never saw.
- **Confirmation e-mail with a personal link.** After booking, the customer gets a confirmation in Swedish or English, with a link to see their booking. The link is personal, expires a week after the visit and works on the salon's own address. E-mails are simulated until the salon's e-mail provider is connected.
- **Cancel or change the time yourself.** Before the salon's free-cancellation deadline, the customer can cancel or move the booking from the link, free of charge. After the deadline, the page shows the salon's phone, e-mail and address instead. Nothing is ever charged or refunded automatically; fees stay the salon's decision in the studio.
- **Privacy by default.** The public page never shows other customers, notes or staff surnames. Every booker is treated the same, so the page never reveals whether a phone number is already a customer. A customer who is erased takes their booking e-mail address with them.
- **Steadier tests.** A flaky admin test that sometimes clicked before the page was ready is fixed.

## What is still needed before real salons go live

- An e-mail provider for the salon's confirmations, and the delivery checks in [#143](https://github.com/Cresavo/cresavo/issues/143).
- A legal review of the short privacy notice on the booking form.
- Production hosting with tested backups, monitoring and an incident routine: the [pilot gate](https://github.com/Cresavo/cresavo/blob/main/docs/QUALITY_SECURITY.md).

## Try it

- In the browser: [demo.cresavo.com](https://demo.cresavo.com). The demo booking flow works as before, in your own browser.
- On your machine (Node.js 22.12+):

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Cresavo/cresavo.git
cd cresavo
npm run demo
```
