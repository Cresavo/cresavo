# Codex night review (gpt-6.2-sol)

An adversarial review of commits `62f104b..ce2474a` on `design/libovo-brand-v09`. Each finding was checked
against the code before it was fixed; all were confirmed. Line numbers refer to the reviewed commit.

### Medium

- `src/app/api/waitlist/route.ts:25`: The limiter trusts the first `X-Forwarded-For` value. On ingress that preserves client-supplied forwarding headers, one client can get fresh buckets by changing the header. The map also resets on cold starts and clears all counters at capacity. Reuse the repo's verified client-address handling and enforce the limit at ingress or in a shared store.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `da19212`. The key now comes from `scripts/client-address.mjs`: the address `scripts/server.mjs` signs (`verifiedClientAddress`), or on Vercel the platform's `x-real-ip` (`vercelClientAddress`, `clientLimitKey`). `X-Forwarded-For` is never read, and requests that cannot be verified share one bucket. At capacity the limiter drops only its oldest windows. The counter stays in process memory, because the public demo has no database. `docs/WAITLIST.md` now says that a platform firewall rule or a shared store must come before a real provider is connected.
- `src/app/api/waitlist/route.ts:13`: The 2,000-character cap is applied after the entire request is buffered, and rate limiting happens after that read. Oversized submissions still consume memory, and a form with valid fields at the front can pass after truncation. Rate-limit before reading and enforce a streaming byte limit that returns 413.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `da19212`. The route checks the limit before it touches the body and cancels the stream of a limited request. The body is read through `readCapped` (`src/concept/site/waitlist-server.ts`). A declared `Content-Length` over 2,000 bytes, or a stream that passes 2,000 bytes, gets 413 with nothing parsed, so a truncated body can no longer pass. The `none` provider still stores and logs nothing.
- `src/concept/assistant/lookups.ts:98`: Staff can ask for another colleague's customer by name and get their next appointment or last treatment even when the customer page denies access. The new lookup also answers owner-only inbox, pricing, and gift-card questions. Apply the studio's role policy before lookup and scope appointment and history queries to the viewer.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. Lookups apply the customer page's rule (`servedBy` in `ops/access.ts`). A staff member who names a customer they do not serve gets "not one of yours" and no card. Appointment and history queries only read the viewer's own bookings. Inbox, price, gift-card and due-back answers are gated by `canOpen` for the page they stand for, and staff get "for the owner". `bookingsReply` also refuses a staff view that has no viewer.
- `src/concept/assistant/lookups.ts:264`: A running-late request with an unrecognized name silently drafts a message to the first active or upcoming customer. Approval would record the simulated message against the wrong person. Ask for clarification when an explicit recipient cannot be resolved and reserve the fallback for an explicit "next customer" request.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. A name that matches nobody gets "I could not find {name}… who should get the text?", and a request with no recipient asks who should get it. Only an explicit "the next customer/client" falls back on whoever is next.
- `src/concept/assistant/lookups.ts:159`: "Can we fit a massage tomorrow at 16:00?" can get a positive answer based on an unrelated morning opening. The lookup parses the date and broad daypart but ignores the requested time. Check the exact slot first and label other openings as alternatives.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. A stated clock time ("16:00", "4pm", "at 16") is checked against `freeStarts` first. The answer is "Yes: … fits … at 16:00" only when that slot is free. Otherwise it says "does not fit … at 16:00. Other free times …:" and lists the times nearest the asked time. Lengths ("60-minute") and dates ("Friday 20 November") are not read as times.
- `src/concept/assistant/engine.ts:241`: With an AI provider configured, the new customer lookups send names and appointment or treatment details in the request's `message` and `draft` fields. The privacy filter only covers prior thread messages. Keep these replies local or redact identifying and sensitive details from every provider-bound field.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. Lookup and booking-list replies are marked `local`, and `use-assistant.ts` never sends them for rewording. As a second layer, `replyRequest` redacts customer names from `message`, `draft`, `facts` and `thread`.
- `src/concept/page/resolve.ts:62`: A tenant host accepts any `/boka/` path, so an unconnected tenant subdomain can display Salong Eko's booking page at `/boka/salong-eko`. The booking footer's relative `/studio` link on a valid tenant host also lands on the unknown-address page. Validate booking paths against the resolved tenant and point the demo-only owner link to the demo origin.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `38adad0`. On a business's address only `/boka/<its slug>` and the paths below it are served. Any other `/boka/…` path gets the unknown-address page. The footer link uses `ownerViewHref`. That is `/studio` on the platform's own hosts, and `demo.<product>/studio` on a business's subdomain or own domain (with the local scheme and port on a dev server).

### Low

- `src/concept/assistant/engine.ts:130`: "Who was here last Friday?" looks forward to the next Friday, and "Who is coming next week?" returns only Monday. Parse past weekdays and week ranges explicitly, and pass both dates to `listBookings`.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. `parseDays` (`intent/dates.ts`) reads "last Friday" and past-tense weekday questions as the previous such day, and reads "this/next/last week" as Monday to Sunday. `bookingsReply` passes `from` and `to` to `list_bookings` and shows the day on each row of a range.
- `src/concept/assistant/lookups.ts:210`: The assistant subtracts redemptions from cancelled bookings, so it reports a lower gift-card balance than the booking page after a cancellation. Reuse `findGiftCard` and `balanceOf`.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. The lookup uses `findGiftCard` and `balanceOf`, so codes match regardless of case and dashes, and a cancelled booking's redemption no longer counts.
- `src/concept/assistant/engine.ts:112`: A requested `100%` offer falls back to `10%`, and `12.5%` is read as `5%`. Parse the full percentage and ask for clarification when it falls outside the supported range.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `8d7f824`. The offer reads the whole number, with decimals ("12.5%", "25 percent"). A value outside `OFFER_PERCENT_RANGE` (1 to 90%, the same ceiling as a campaign) gets a question and no proposal.
- `src/concept/site/site.module.css:209`: The empty waitlist `role="status"` is hidden with `display: none`, then revealed in the same update that adds the result. Screen readers can miss the announcement, especially when success removes the focused form. Keep the live region mounted in the accessibility tree and move focus to the result when removing the form.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `3ee5f4e`. The status line stays mounted and visible to assistive technology while empty (zero height, not `display: none`). When the answer replaces the form after a submit, focus moves to the answer (`tabIndex={-1}`).
- `src/pages/landing.tsx:19`: Existing `/en/landing` links now return 404 because the English landing route was removed. Add a permanent redirect to `/landing`.
  - **Resolution:** fixed in `ddc2c35`. `next.config.ts` redirects `/en/landing` to `/landing` permanently (308).
