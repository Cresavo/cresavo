# Waitlist offers

Waitlist offers are part of the **Waiting list** add-on (`waitlist` in `src/concept/modules/registry.ts`). New salons start with the add-on off. Once it is on, offers default to **Ask me first**, and owners opt in to **Automatic**. The add-on adds no Settings section and no More entry. Its page lives under Customers at `/studio/customers/waitlist` and is the owner's alone: the staff view cannot open it (`canOpen`). Customers, Today's free-times card and a cancelled visit in the Inbox link to it. API resources `waitlist`, `waitlist-offers` and `waitlist-rules` are declared in the module's `api` entry. All three are planned.

## What customers do

- **Join.** When a day is full, the booking page offers "Notify me if something opens up". The customer chooses up to seven days (`MAX_REQUEST_DAYS`, also enforced by the reducer). They can add a practitioner when more than one person does the treatment. The default is any time on the chosen day; a narrower window applies to every chosen day, from the earliest start to the latest finish. Joining again with the same treatment, practitioner, days and window is refused. An older plain waiting-list flag becomes the new request and keeps its place in the queue.
- **Answer an offer.** The simulated text links to the salon's own booking page (`/boka/<slug>?offer=<id>`), which stays white-label. Opening the link changes nothing. **Book this time** rechecks the time under the booking lock, and the claim carries the price and terms the page showed. The page shows the full slot summary, including when online changes are already closed. A claim on changed terms is refused and the page shows the new ones. When a deposit is due, it opens the normal deposit checkout with the offer's own deadline. **No thanks**, leaving checkout or missing the deadline hands the time to the next customer.
- **Leave.** Every offer page and My bookings can remove all of the customer's requests. My bookings can also remove one. A request ends at salon midnight after its last day.
- Joining the waiting list creates a contact, so `isNewBookingCustomer` keeps first-visit deposit terms until that contact actually books.

## What the owner sees

- **How offers are sent.** A segmented control switches between Off, Ask me first and Automatic. Time to answer can be 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes (15 by default). **Pause offers** stops new sends. Pausing, Off and switching the add-on off all honour offers already sent. Automatic texts wait from 21:00 until 08:00 salon time. A gap is dropped only when nothing in it still starts after 08:00. The owner can still offer a time by hand at night, including one waiting for 08:00.
- **Offers out now** lists each held time with its countdown, a demo badge and the personal link.
- **Freed times to offer** lists gaps waiting for approval and the next customer in line. **Offer to next in line** approves a gap; later offers for it go out automatically. A gap held for quiet hours says so.
- **Offer another free time** lets the owner start a queue for time that was already free. Only the freed parts of an existing queue (or all of an opening the owner chose) are left out of it.
- **Waiting customers** and **Earlier offers** show the queue in order and every ended offer with its outcome.
- Cancelling a visit in the studio while in Ask me first mode can approve its freed time at once with the **Offer the slot to the waiting list** switch. In the command box, "cancel … and offer the slot to the waiting list" does the same. Only the owner can give this approval: staff proposals leave it out, and the log capture refuses it unless the owner acted or approved an assistant's draft (`approvedBy: "owner"`, set by the Inbox, the server and the studio assistant when the owner confirms).
- Treatments can have preparation and cleanup time ("More options" in the treatment sheet), from 0 to 120 minutes. The person and the room or chair are busy for it, but the customer is not. Online booking never offers a start whose preparation has begun, and an offer's hold and every deposit checkout end when preparation would start. A claim keeps the room or chair the offer reserved through checkout and booking, and the booking always belongs to the offered customer by id, whoever else shares their phone number. Moving a visit keeps the length and preparation it was booked with, and moving never offers the waiting list: an offer books a new visit and cannot stand in for the move. Offered online starts use the exact moment preparation would begin. The calendar and Today warn when a schedule change leaves it outside working hours.

## How it works

- **Matching** (`src/concept/booking/waitlist-match.ts`) takes the state and an explicit instant. The longest-waiting customer goes first. Ties go to the earliest requested day, then the entry id. A customer is offered each gap once and holds at most one offer at a time. A gap that fits only customers who hold another offer, or that a checkout holds for now, stays open and is offered when that offer or checkout ends. The customer whose cancellation freed the time is skipped. A match needs the treatment, an optional practitioner, a free room or chair, preparation and cleanup inside the gap and the visit inside the customer's window. It also needs the visit to start after the current time and the customer to have no other visit then. Preferences use salon wall time; holds and treatment lengths use elapsed minutes. A repeated wall time uses its first occurrence. A visit or buffer that crosses a clock change is skipped.
- **Released time** is found after a cancellation or move, and after longer working or opening hours. Only days someone waits for are checked. The gap is each free stretch the freed time touches, with the part of the freed time inside it, so a cancelled half hour next to a free half hour can fill an hour, but a match must use some of the freed time. Separate freed parts of one stretch stay separate (`released` is a list). The freed start itself is tried first. Bookable starts are worked out once per treatment and gap, then each customer's window applies.
- **The queue** (`waitlist-offer.ts`) runs in the reducer after every change that touches bookings, hours, staff, services, holds or the waiting list. Before an action that finds a hold ended or quiet hours over, it settles at the same instant without sending anything. Settling drops passed requests and lapses offers that ended or can no longer be booked, including when the room or chair an offer holds no longer suits the treatment. After the action it records newly freed gaps and sends the next offer where the rules allow, so a withdrawal, erasure or pause in that action is respected. An open offer whose gap is missing (after an undo) gets it back. `waitlist/advance` runs it on the salon's clock. An open offer holds its person and its room or chair for everyone else (`activeHolds` in `slots.ts`).
- **Actions** (`waitlist-actions.ts`, `actions.ts`) are `waitlist/rules`, `waitlist/offer`, `waitlist/advance`, `online/waitlist` (with `days` and `window`), `online/claim`, `online/decline` and `online/leave`. `booking/cancel` takes `offerWaitingList`. Only the owner can change the rules or approve a gap (`OWNER_ONLY` in `log/capture.ts`). Customer decisions and the automatic handover each get an activity entry with their actor (`automatic`, source `waitlist`), also when the action that caused them is not logged itself. These entries, and the main entries for joining, claiming, declining and leaving, carry the customer's id, so erasure empties them. The claim's notice is in the audit redaction map (`PERSONAL_FIELDS` and `prisma/grants/erase-subject.sql`). A change that sent a text cannot be undone.
- **Texts** go through `sendDemoMessage` inside the reducer, so they use whatever channels the store staged (`withDemoChannels`). The memory and Postgres stores publish them only after the commit. The browser store's `dispatchWithLog` now stages messages as well as payments and records them after its save. A discarded attempt or a refused apply sends nothing. The text's idempotency reference is the offer id. The `StudioStore` contract is unchanged.
- **Time** comes from the reducer's `now` and `withSlotClock`. Stores pass their trusted clock, and the browser store passes its own. Nothing in matching or the queue reads the wall clock. While a studio or booking page is open, the browser store schedules `waitlist/advance` for the next deadline (`nextWaitlistWake`). It also runs on focus and on opening. With every tab closed nothing runs, and the first page opened catches up. Every save in the browser store goes through the booking lock when the browser has Web Locks, offer or not, so no two tabs ever write their whole snapshot over each other. A change made outside the lock (`dispatch`, `dispatchWithLog`) shows in that tab at once and is committed under the lock right after, on the latest saved copy; a commit that is then refused is reported by a toast and the page shows what was saved again. Each change shown before its commit is also written at once to a per-tab journal in localStorage; the commit removes it, and if the page goes away first (a reload or a link to another page) the next page in the same tab commits what is left, skipping any operation whose log entry is already saved. Callers that show or open what a change created, or confirm success to a customer, await `dispatchCommitted` (or `dispatchConfirmed`) and read the result from that commit: selling a gift card, launching a campaign, joining the waiting list, a review and a test message. Remaining risk: a browser without Web Locks still writes directly, re-reading storage just before each save, and in that browser no personal offer is sent or booked. Undo on a change made outside the lock refers to its operation id (`LogMeta.op`, the same on the preview and the commit), so it always takes back this tab's own change. Records a change creates get ids from that operation (`recordId` in `data/ids.ts`), the same in the preview and the commit, so an edit made from the preview reaches this tab's record and never another tab's. Assistant cards say approved or undone only after the commit. An assistant proposal approved in the Inbox is reported to the server only after its commit. Offers are sent and claimed only inside the booking lock, which needs Web Locks. A browser without them sends and books no personal offers and says so on the waiting list and the offer page. A change previewed outside the lock sends nothing; its locked commit sends what is due. A change that sends a personal offer, a claim, an offer's deposit payment, leaving its checkout, a decline, a withdrawal, a new request, every owner queue decision (including approval while cancelling) and switching the add-on on or off must reach shared storage, or it is refused, nothing is sent and the customer is not told it worked. A gap that could be offered right away but has no offer out counts as due, so a failed first send is retried. A tick that cannot save or cannot get the lock backs off from 30 seconds to 5 minutes, and focus retries at once. A sample salon keeps its schedule across midnight only while a gap is still ahead, an offer is still held or a visitor's request still has days ahead.
- **Data.** `DemoState` gains `waitlistEntries`, `waitlistOffers`, `waitlistGaps` and `waitlistRules`. Services and bookings gain optional `bufferBefore` and `bufferAfter`. Version 24 migrates every customer with the older waiting-list flag to an entry with no treatment or days (`legacyEntries`). Nobody's consent is guessed and nobody is texted until they choose their days. Paired assistant workspaces saved earlier get empty waitlist fields when read. Deleting a customer keeps their entries in the trash bundle. Erasing them removes their entries and offers.
- **Not ported from the first draft:** recording a pre-start absence and shortening a visit as release triggers. Both touch the no-show rules, which have changed since, and need their own design.

## Before real texts

Move matching, clocks and holds into the booking engine. Atomically reserve staff and resources and consume a single-use, tenant-scoped claim link at booking. Offer ids are derived from the salon, gap, entry and instant, which is enough for a browser-local demo but not for a real link. Queue provider delivery after the reservation commits, with idempotent sends, delivery receipts, retry deadlines and an opt-out check before each send.

- **GDPR.** Document the processing purpose and lawful basis. Minimize stored contact details, define retention for expired entries and delivery history, and test access and deletion. [IMY guidance](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/grundlaggande-principer/)
- **Marketing Practices Act (marknadsföringslagen).** Record the customer's request for availability texts and honour withdrawal. Obtain separate prior consent for promotional texts unless a statutory exception applies. Include a working opt-out in every marketing message. [Sections 19–20](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)
- **Consumer Services Act (konsumenttjänstlagen).** Personal treatments such as haircuts generally fall outside its scope. Review distance-booking withdrawal rights, deposit terms and reasonable cancellation charges for each service before enabling payments. [Konsumentverket guidance](https://www.konsumentverket.se/varor-och-tjanster/kopa-skonhetsbehandling-tips/)
