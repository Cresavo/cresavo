# Owner studio polish audit

The first ten fixes address mistakes an owner could make during a booking, barriers to operating the studio, and dead ends with a small, local repair. The ranking weighs impact on the salon, frequency and confidence in the proposed fix. Effort is **S** for a local change and **M** for a change that spans screens, state or draft handling. Source references use repository-relative paths and line numbers at `36255cc3df70`, before the fixes.

The saved desktop views show a clear schedule in both themes. They also show the salon name and demo notice competing for one line in the sidebar. The 390px Swedish phone view gives the next customer priority, with Profile, Move and Call side by side. The first-run phone view shows a button labelled Calendar when the owner is ready to take the first booking. These are historical captures. Findings for the other screens come from following the components, their data and responsive styles. ## Visual polish pass (November 2026)

A look-and-feel pass over Today, Calendar, Customers, Inbox, More and the new-booking sheet, built by Claude and critiqued from screenshots by an independent model over three rounds. The last round reported no High or Medium findings. The component rules it introduced are in [DESIGN_SYSTEM](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

Left open, all Low:

- **New booking, desktop.** The footer clips the first row of date buttons under Time, and the longest treatment name wraps to three lines. Widen the desktop sheet.
- **Calendar week.** When only one person works a day, the Lunch label is centred on the day, not on the hatched column.
- **Today, next customer.** A routine customer note uses the warning triangle. Decide whether notes should use neutral styling.
- **Today, first visit.** The tour still sits above the next-customer card. It takes keyboard focus, so moving it needs a behaviour decision.
