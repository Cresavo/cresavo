Stock shows what is left of each product and when it runs out. When a visit is marked done, the products it uses are deducted.

To order, open the purchase list, adjust the amounts and approve: one email draft per supplier is saved in the Inbox (simulated). When the delivery arrives, tap Goods received to add it to the stock. Use Sell a product to sell over the counter.

Say "order more foot cream" in the command box for a quick start.
