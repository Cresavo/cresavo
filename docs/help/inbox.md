Everything from customers lands in Inbox: booking requests, reschedule and cancel requests, reviews and messages.

Open a message and a reply is already drafted. Edit it and press Send reply. If the customer asked for a time, Send and book also creates the booking. Mark all as read clears the unread badge.

Changes proposed by an AI assistant you connected (Claude, ChatGPT) show at the top under Waiting for your approval. Nothing changes until you press Approve; the change is then listed in What happened? with Undo.

Replies in the demo are simulated: nothing is really sent.
