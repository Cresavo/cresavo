Ask the Help button first: it answers from these articles and can show you where to click.

If that is not enough, choose Create a ticket. It attaches a short outline of the screen you are on, the page, a request number and your last 20 changes, so you do not have to explain what happened. You see exactly what is attached before it is saved. Tickets are listed under What happened, on the Support tickets tab.
