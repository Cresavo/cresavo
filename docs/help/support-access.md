If you need hands-on help, you can give support read-only access for a limited time.

1. Open More, then Settings, and find Support access.
2. Choose 1 hour or 24 hours.
3. Stop access whenever you like.

Support can only look, never change anything. Every grant and every stop is saved in What happened and in the list under Support access, so you always see who had access and when.
