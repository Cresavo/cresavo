Your data is yours. Open More, then Your data & AI and choose Export: everything as JSON, or customers or bookings as CSV for a spreadsheet. It is free and always available.

The same page connects Claude or ChatGPT over MCP: copy the command or config for your assistant. It can read bookings, free times, customers (names only) and figures, and propose changes. Proposals wait in Inbox under Waiting for your approval until you approve or decline them.
