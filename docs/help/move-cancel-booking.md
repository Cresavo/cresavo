Tap a booking in the Calendar to open it. Choose Move to see the free times, or Cancel booking to give a reason.

On a computer you can also drag a booking to another time in the day view. When you cancel, you can offer the free slot to customers on the waiting list (simulated text message).

A cancelled booking waits in the Trash for 30 days, so you can bring it back.
