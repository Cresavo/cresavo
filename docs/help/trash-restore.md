Cancelled bookings and deleted customers are not gone at once. They stay in the Trash for 30 days.

1. Open More, then What happened, and choose the Trash tab.
2. Find the item. It shows how many days are left.
3. Tap Restore.

A booking can only be restored if its time is still free. A restored customer comes back with their bookings, journal and history. To delete a customer, open their card and choose Delete customer.
