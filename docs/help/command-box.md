Press Ask {assistant} (or Ctrl+K, ⌘K on a Mac) and write or say what you want, for example "Move Anna's appointment on Thursday to 14:00" or "How much did I make this month?". On a phone the button sits above the menu; the microphone next to it starts listening right away.

{assistant} shows what it understood as a proposal. Nothing changes until you press the approve button. After approving you can undo it from the same message, or later under What happened.
