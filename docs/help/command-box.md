The box at the top of every page does things for you. Write or say what you want, for example "Move Anna's appointment on Thursday to 14:00" or "How much did I make this month?".

Flerdo shows what it understood as a proposal. Nothing changes until you press the approve button. After approving you can undo it from the message that appears, or later under What happened.
