Press {assistant} (or Ctrl+K, ⌘K on a Mac) and write or say what you want, for example "Who is next?", "Move Anna's appointment on Thursday to 14:00" or "How much did I make this month?". On a phone the button sits above the menu; the microphone is inside the panel.

A question gets its answer straight from your data. A change is shown as a proposal: nothing happens until you press the approve button. After approving you can undo it from the same message, or later under What happened.
