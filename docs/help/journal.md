Open a customer and choose the Journal tab. Press Dictate entry and tell what you did and saw; Flerdo sorts it into treatment, observations and advice for you to check and save.

Every edit keeps the earlier version. Journal text is never shown in What happened or in logs: only that an entry was added or edited.

If you do not need journals, switch the Journal module off under Features.
