# Public launch checklist

v0.1 supplies design masters for implementation and internal review. Public launch requires named owners and evidence for the items below. The research, releases and sign-offs in this list are pending.

## Name and identity · product owner

- [ ] Record a professional trademark search for Cresavo, similar spellings, soundalikes and the proposed device mark in each launch territory. Scope the search with counsel across Nice classes **9, 42 and 35** and the actual software, hosting and business services offered. Record the territories, relevant goods/services, findings and decision to proceed.
- [ ] Check company names, app stores, competing products and domains for confusion. Test pronunciation, spelling from speech and unintended associations with English and Swedish speakers, then with speakers in each target market.
- [ ] Confirm control of cresavo.com and the demo subdomain. Verify HTTPS, redirects and both demo paths on the intended host before publishing links.
- [ ] Verify and reserve the agreed social handles, including LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube, and relevant GitHub organisation and package names. Keep a dated record of ownership and unavailable names.
- [ ] Approve the English line, its Swedish counterpart and “Ask Cresavo”. Review the four outlined wordmarks on real devices and in print. Inspect the c aperture, a counter and vo spacing at the minimum size.
- [ ] Decide the final small symbol or approve a dedicated small-size lettermark. Replace the temporary c, then export and check favicon, maskable/install icons, social avatar and open-graph artwork in their real crops.

## Product and copy · design and engineering

- [ ] Adopt the kit at the platform theme import and brand configuration. Update visible names, accessible names, page titles, support copy, manifest, icons and social previews. Verify screenshot crops against the delivered build. Preserve stored studio state, tenant palettes and integration identifiers through the rename.
- [ ] Compare CSS and JSON tokens, test first paint with the saved theme opposite the OS, and check browser chrome after a theme switch. Run contrast checks on rendered text, hover, pressed, focus, disabled controls, divider lines, charts and status messages. Recheck on tenant palettes and neutral defaults.
- [ ] Complete owner booking, proposal → edit/approve → undo, and customer booking by keyboard and touch. Verify focus return and the last action above tabs, safe areas and the mobile keyboard. With assistance off, the same core workflows must remain reachable.
- [ ] Inspect 320, 390, 768 and 1280 px in both schemes. Recheck 390 px at 200% zoom, especially the customer card, Finance, Features and the booking editor identified in the previous QA. Verify full names and labelled staff identities. Run screen-reader checks and real Safari/iOS and Chrome/Android tests alongside desktop Firefox/WebKit.
- [ ] Visit the tenant page, widget, gift card, My bookings, confirmation and unknown address with the studio set to the opposite appearance. Check the page canvas, tab icon, install metadata, embed title, errors and attribution as well as the flow itself.
- [ ] Verify font licensing and self-hosted files, screenshot and tenant-image rights, and fictional details. Check both site pages with images unavailable and with text enlarged. Keep customer details and real email collection out of the demo.
- [ ] Review claims against shipping capabilities. Label demo finance, communications and publishing as simulated where shown. Clear privacy, contact and consent copy before enabling any future live sign-up or messaging. Record product-owner approval of the actual site build before public launch.
