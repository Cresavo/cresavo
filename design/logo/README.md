An open door gives “More time. On your terms.” a familiar shape. The arch borrows its curve from the wordmark’s lowercase c, while a low sill and a door swung forward give it depth. At 16 px the diagonal strip of daylight still separates the door from its frame. Beside the name, the door extends a little above and below the letters, giving the two pieces similar visual weight.

## Placement

- **Clear space.** For either lockup, leave at least half the visible height of the final o on all four sides, measured from the ink. For a free-standing symbol, leave a quarter of its visible height. The icon and avatar exports carry their own padding.
- **Minimum size.** Use the symbol or favicon down to 16 × 16 CSS px. Use the horizontal lockup at 124 CSS px wide or larger and the stacked lockup at 98 CSS px or larger. Both sizes keep the wordmark above its 96 px minimum. In print, allow 32 mm for the horizontal lockup or 26 mm for the stacked one.
- **Do** keep the door on the right with the sill below the opening. Use the supplied lockups when the name and symbol appear together. Set the tagline separately.
- **Avoid** mirroring the door, closing its diagonal gap, retyping the name, or adding shadows, outlines or gradients. Use a solid ground behind the mark.
