# A1 · Pen note

An unjoined, hand-lettered signature. The heavier downstroke and fine upturn of the opening **c** give the hand a direction. The **e** repeats that movement, while the single-storey **a** lets the middle of the name breathe.

The favicon, app icon and avatar all use the first contour of the wordmark. At 16 px the two ends of the **c** stay apart. Its slant and rising tail carry more personality in the small booking credit than they do in the initial by itself.
