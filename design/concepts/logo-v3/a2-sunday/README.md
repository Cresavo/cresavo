# A2 · Sunday

An upright, hand-drawn lowercase with a double-storey **a**, a gently rising crossbar on the **e** and a small inward turn at the top of the **c**. The open spacing makes this the easiest name to read in the booking footer.

The first **c** is also the favicon, app icon and avatar. Its opening holds at 16 px. The small turn at its tip is easier to appreciate on the app icon, and the lone initial is the least distinctive of these four marks.
