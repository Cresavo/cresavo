# B2 · Palm

A cupped hand in profile carries the idea of personal service. The turn at the centre suggests a thumb. An open gap at the wrist keeps the drawing light alongside Sunday's upright letters.

The symbol fills the favicon, app icon and avatar. The thumb and the lower curve separate at 16 px, though the gesture is much easier to identify at 32 px. The hand feels particularly at home with massage and hands-on care.
